The last post on this blog went up in September 2014. It was about learning Git.

In the decade since, I stopped writing and kept working — mostly on Kubernetes.
I co-authored [kustomize][kustomize] and [kubebuilder][kubebuilder], and more
recently [kubectl-ai][kubectl-ai], an AI agent for Kubernetes. These days I'm on
the GKE team at Google Cloud.

I'm starting again. The plan is to write about what I'm actually working on:
Kubernetes internals, the operational reality of running GKE at scale, and what
happens when you point LLM agents at infrastructure — which is turning out to be
a stranger problem than I expected.

Everything I wrote between 2009 and 2014 is still here, in the [archive][archive].
It's a snapshot of a different era — Redis when it was new, CoffeeScript,
Backbone.js, MongoDB backups to S3, a fair amount of Scala. The technical details
have aged badly. I'm leaving them up anyway.
